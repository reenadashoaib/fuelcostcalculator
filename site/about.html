About Fuel Cost Calculator

Fuel Cost Calculator is a free tool for working out what a car journey costs in fuel before you set off. It's built for road trips, commutes, school runs and splitting costs with friends.

How it works

You enter the distance, your car's fuel economy and the price of fuel. The calculator converts everything to litres and kilometres, works out the fuel the trip burns, and multiplies that by the price. It handles L/100 km, km/L, US MPG and UK MPG, and prices per litre or per gallon.

How accurate is it?

The maths is exact for the numbers you enter. Real trips vary: traffic, weather, hills, speed and load all change how much fuel a car uses. Treat the result as a good estimate and add a margin for longer trips.

Your data

The calculation runs entirely in your browser. We don't receive or store the numbers you type. See the privacy policy for how advertising on this site uses cookies.
